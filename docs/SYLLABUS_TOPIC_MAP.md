# Syllabus Topic Map: Lower Secondary G3 (Sec 1–2)

*Source: MOE syllabus PDFs James attached on 29 Sep 2026, matching the versions listed on MOE's curriculum page that day. Science: 2023 G2/G3 Lower Secondary Science (57 pp, file dated Jan 2026). History: 2021 Lower Secondary G2/G3 History, version May 2024 (37 pp). Geography: 2021 Lower Secondary G2/G3 Geography (44 pp). All three are two-year courses; MOE does not split topics into Sec 1 and Sec 2, schools do. Year splits below are inferred from her Sec 1 exam papers and must be confirmed with her.*

## How the app uses this

- Each topic becomes a node in the app's topic tree (subject → level → topic → subtopic).
- Syllabus content marked optional for G2 or Normal (Academic) is **examinable for G3**, so the app treats it as core.
- The school also adds topics that are not in the syllabus (e.g. "Weather and Climate" in her Sec 1 Geography exam), so the tree must allow school-added topics as well as "not taught" ones.
- Useful built-in material to ingest from the PDFs:
  - **Science glossary of command words** (state, describe, explain, compare, infer…, 23 terms), the basis for question-reading hints and for teaching answer length ("state" = concise; "explain" = give reasons).
  - **Geography "Main Terms"** listed for every guiding question, a ready-made key-term list for marking and compact notes.
  - Science learning outcomes (about 170 bullets) and History sub-topics, to tag questions and generate syllabus-aligned questions.
