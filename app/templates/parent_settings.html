Study level

She is offered only topics at this level, in Today's session, Choose my own, Revise, Practice, the year-end review and the map. In Revise and Practice she can switch level for that visit. A topic you mark Not taught above stays hidden. Switching level never changes her progress, and the dashboard and session log always show every level.

Today's session

Cards with a single point (definitions, single facts) are grouped into a separate Quick-fire step of up to 5.

These caps stop a step running long when her questions are short. A session also ends by itself, after the item she is on, once its planned length has passed (time spent idle or on a break does not count). She can press Keep going to carry on.
